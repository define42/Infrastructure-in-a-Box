Connect with trust

  1. Download the certificate and compare its SHA-256 fingerprint with the copy provided by your network administrator or on the gateway host.
  2. Import it into your device’s trusted root certificate authorities. Some browsers use a separate certificate store.
  3. Reopen this gateway at https://{{.Hostname}}/. Once the root is trusted, your browser can verify the HTTPS certificate.

Trusting this root allows it to authenticate certificates it signs. Verify the fingerprint through a trusted source before installing it; this page alone cannot establish initial trust.
